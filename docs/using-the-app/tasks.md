# Tasks

Beekeeping is full of small jobs that are easy to forget: add a super before the flow, fit a clearer board the day before extraction, treat for varroa once the honey is off. Tasks are Openbeehive's reminders: a title and an optional due date. Works offline; see [Offline and sync](./offline-and-sync.md).

## Creating a task

Open **Tasks** (or choose **+ New → New task** on the Overview). At the top, type what needs doing, optionally pick a due date, and tap **Add**.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Title | A short description of the job, e.g. "Add second brood box". Put the hive or apiary in the title if it matters. |
| Due | The day the task is due. Optional. |

That is the whole task. There are no notes, no link to a hive or apiary, and no recurrence.

:::tip
Create a task without a due date for "someday" jobs, such as "Build two more nuc boxes over winter".
:::

Tasks you create in the app are **personal**: only you see them, on all your devices, and only in the workspace you created them in. Other members of a shared workspace do not see your list, and after switching workspaces you see the tasks you created there.

## Open vs done

Every task is either **open** or **done**.

- Tap the box on the left of a task to tick it off. Done tasks move into the **Completed (n)** section below the open ones, which starts collapsed, and are not counted as open.
- Tap the box again to reopen a task you ticked off by mistake.
- The pencil edits the title or due date; the bin deletes the task. After a delete, a bar offers **Undo** for a few seconds.
- A task whose due date has passed is marked **Overdue**.

If you tick off the same task on two of your devices, the task ends up done.

## Tasks on the Overview

The **Open tasks** tile counts tasks that are not done. **Upcoming tasks** lists up to five open tasks with their due dates; tasks whose due date has passed are marked **Overdue**, and tapping a task opens it in the Tasks view. See [The dashboard](./dashboard.md).

:::tip
For recurring jobs, add the next task when you tick off the current one. After a seven-day swarm check, add "Swarm check" for the following week.
:::

## Examples of typical beekeeping tasks

**Spring**

- First full inspection on a mild day.
- Add a queen excluder and the first super before the main flow.
- Check feed stores after a cold snap.

**Early summer**

- Inspect every 7 to 9 days during swarm season.
- Add or remove supers as the flow dictates.
- Prepare nuc boxes for swarm control or splits.

**Late summer / harvest**

- Fit clearer boards the day before extraction.
- Remove and extract honey supers.
- Begin varroa treatment once the honey is off.

**Autumn**

- Feed for winter and check overall stores.
- Fit mouse guards before the first frosts.
- Oxalic acid treatment in the broodless period (timing varies by region and method).

**Winter**

- Heft hives to gauge stores; feed fondant if light.
- Clear snow from entrances after heavy falls.
- Repair and build equipment ready for spring.

:::caution
Treatment timing and the products you may legally use vary by country and region. Follow your local guidance and the product label. See [Varroa](../beekeeping/varroa.md).
:::
